Support

How can we help?

For app issues, account questions, subscription help, privacy requests, or feedback, contact Fameally support.

Contact

Email support@fameally.com and include as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.

  • App issue Tell us what happened, what you expected, your app version, device model, and iOS or Android version.
  • Account or sign-in help Contact us from the email address linked to your Fameally account where possible.
  • Subscriptions Billing, renewals, and cancellations are managed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We can still help explain where to manage your subscription.
  • Privacy or data request Use the same support email and include the request type in the subject line.

Account deletion

You can request account deletion from inside the app, or use the public account deletion page.

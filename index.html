Why Fameally

A calmer way to get from "what are we eating?" to "the list is ready."

Fameally focuses on the practical weekly routine: create meals with ingredients, place them into a plan, generate the food shop, then keep the rest of the household list organised alongside it.

Features

Designed around real household planning

Plan meals quickly

Add meals to the week and keep breakfast, lunch, and dinner planning as simple or detailed as you need.

Generate food lists

Ingredients from planned meals flow into your shopping list, with matching units counted together automatically.

Organise the whole shop

Keep food, cleaning, toiletry, and general items in separate lists so shopping stays easier to scan.

Reuse regular weeks

Save meal plans, keep weekly regulars in your library, and bring familiar routines back faster.

Share a family workspace

Invite household members and keep shared meals, plans, library items, and shopping lists in sync.

Recover recent changes

Restore deleted meals, ingredients, plans, methods, and list items when something is removed by mistake.
